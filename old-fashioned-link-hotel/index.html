More from the junior lab

The Old Fashioned Link Hotel is a made-up hotel from the MayTech Labs junior lab: ideas our youngest founder has had since he was two, brought to life for fun. The name, the massages, the room service, the free delivery from any of the restaurants, and a room too luxurious to leave are his; everything else here we made up around it. It doesn't exist, has no rooms, and any resemblance to a real hotel is a happy accident.
