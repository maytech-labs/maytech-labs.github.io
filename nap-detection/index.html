    Why it exists

    Backup, for the hardest part of the afternoon.

    Getting a kid down for a nap can be the hardest part of the day. They aren't tired. They were never tired. They'll explain at length exactly how not tired they are, and when a parent says it's nap time, that's only the opening offer in a negotiation.

    It goes better with a second opinion. Not from a parent, who clearly has an agenda, but from software: an impartial system that checks the nap database, asks Santa Claus, consults the Cookie Monster, and dials grandma before it rules. When the advanced nap-detection algorithms come back with a yes, it isn't us saying it anymore. It's the science.

    How it decides

    A rigorous, three-step process, every time.

    1. The consultation

      Six experts weigh in, drawn from a panel that includes Santa Claus, grandma, the moon, and the sheep. It's a little different each time, so it never feels rehearsed.

    2. The verdict

      An official, stamped result with a case number and 100% confidence. Appeals are welcome and reviewed by the teddy bear. They have all been denied.

    3. Lights out

      The whole screen becomes a quiet night sky with a moon and a nap timer. Tap anywhere, or press Escape, when it's time to wake up.
