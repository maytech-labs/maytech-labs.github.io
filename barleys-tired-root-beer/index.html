Our story

Founded at age two. Still growing.

  1. The beginning. One root beer, one big idea, and a founder who could barely reach the counter.
  2. Going artisanal. Root beer was going so well that cream sodas were the obvious next step.
  3. Branching out. Sarsaparilla joins the family, because every soda company needs one.
  4. Today. Clean Root takes over as the flagship. The root beer is still tired.

Where can I buy some?

Nowhere. Barley's Tired Root Beer Company is pretend, and so is the soda. We're sorry, too.

More from the junior lab

Barley's Tired Root Beer Company is a made-up company from the MayTech Labs junior lab: ideas our youngest founder has had since he was two, brought to life for fun. It doesn't exist, sells nothing, and any resemblance to real sodas is a happy accident.
