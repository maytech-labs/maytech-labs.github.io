More from the junior lab

Tilde House is a made-up restaurant from the MayTech Labs junior lab: ideas our youngest founder has had since he was two, brought to life for fun. The name, the fine dining, and the tilde-shaped food and glasses are his; the menu and everything else here we made up around it. It doesn't exist, serves nothing, and any resemblance to a real restaurant is a happy accident.
