More from the junior lab

The H Diner is a made-up restaurant from the MayTech Labs junior lab: ideas our youngest founder has had since he was two, brought to life for fun. The name is his; the menu and everything else here we made up around it. It doesn't exist, serves nothing, and any resemblance to a real restaurant is a happy accident.
