What it does

Three parts of collecting, in one place.

  1. Binders

    Sign in with Google and build public binders, each with its own link. Every card records its set, condition, foil treatment, and whether it's for trade, for sale, or both.

  2. Foil treatments

    Cards are drawn in the finish they actually have, from traditional foil to galaxy, surge, and Pokémon holo, with the shine moving as you look. You can turn the motion off.

  3. The trading desk

    Keep a public wanted list with priorities, send and receive offers, accept or decline them, and talk each trade through in its own thread.
