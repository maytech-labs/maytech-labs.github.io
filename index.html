A tiny pet that lives in your terminal. It hatches from an egg, grows up over a week, and gets hungry, sleepy, and messy in real time, even while it's closed. Feed it, clean up after it, and play five minigames to earn coins for treats.

It can sit in your shell prompt too, so you'll know when it's hungry.
